## Idea 1: "Atmospheric Altitude" – Aerospace Editorial Design

**Design Movement:** Editorial/Magazine-style meets aerospace engineering aesthetics. Inspired by high-end aviation publications like Monocle and Bloomberg Businessweek, combined with technical aviation instrument design.

**Core Principles:**
1. Dramatic negative space with content anchored to asymmetric grids
2. Cinematic full-bleed imagery with typographic overlays
3. Data-driven storytelling through animated counters and infographics
4. Horizontal scroll sections for service showcases

**Color Philosophy:** Deep navy (#0B1D3A) as primary ground color, representing altitude and trust. Accent with electric teal (#00D4AA) for sustainability signals. Warm white (#F8F6F3) for content areas. The palette evokes cockpit instruments at night – professional, precise, trustworthy.

**Layout Paradigm:** Magazine-style editorial layout with alternating full-width cinematic sections and offset two-column content blocks. Key sections use a "split-screen" approach – image on one side, content offset on the other. Services presented in a horizontal scroll carousel.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Thin horizontal rules and aviation-inspired waypoint markers as section dividers
2. Floating metric cards with glassmorphism effect showing impact numbers
3. Diagonal clip-path transitions between sections

**Interaction Philosophy:** Scroll-triggered reveals with staggered animations. Parallax depth on hero imagery. Magnetic cursor effects on CTAs. Smooth section snapping.

**Animation:** Content fades in from bottom with 0.6s ease-out. Numbers count up when entering viewport. Partner logos slide in continuously. Section transitions use subtle parallax layers.

**Typography System:** Display: "Space Grotesk" (700) for headlines – geometric, modern, technical. Body: "DM Sans" (400/500) for readability. Monospace accents: "JetBrains Mono" for data/metrics.

## Idea 2: "Green Meridian" – Organic Tech Minimalism

**Design Movement:** Scandinavian minimalism meets biophilic design. Inspired by brands like Stripe, Linear, and Nordic sustainability companies. Clean, spacious, with organic touches.

**Core Principles:**
1. Ultra-clean whitespace with content breathing room
2. Subtle organic shapes (leaf-inspired curves) as background elements
3. Card-based modular layout for services
4. Micro-interactions that feel alive and responsive

**Color Philosophy:** Crisp white (#FFFFFF) base with soft sage green (#2D6A4F) as primary brand color. Light mint (#D8F3DC) for subtle backgrounds. Charcoal (#1B1B1B) for text. The palette feels fresh, trustworthy, and environmentally conscious without being cliché.

**Layout Paradigm:** Vertical rhythm with generous spacing. Content blocks alternate between centered narrow-width text and wider card grids. Services section uses a bento-grid layout with varying card sizes. Sticky navigation with progress indicator.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Soft blob shapes as background accents that subtly animate
2. Rounded card components with soft shadows and hover lift effects
3. A thin green progress bar in the navbar showing scroll position

**Interaction Philosophy:** Gentle hover states with scale and shadow changes. Smooth scroll with intersection observer reveals. Cards lift on hover. CTAs have a satisfying press animation.

**Animation:** Fade-up with slight scale (0.95 → 1.0) on scroll entry. Staggered card reveals. Smooth counter animations. Blob backgrounds drift slowly.

**Typography System:** Display: "Outfit" (600/700) – modern geometric sans-serif. Body: "Inter" (400/500) for maximum readability. Clean, professional, no-nonsense.

## Idea 3: "Carbon Blueprint" – Technical Precision Design

**Design Movement:** Swiss International Style meets dark-mode SaaS aesthetics. Inspired by Vercel, Raycast, and technical documentation design. Grid-obsessed, data-forward, premium.

**Core Principles:**
1. Dark-mode-first with strategic light accent areas
2. Precise grid system with visible structure lines as design elements
3. Technical diagrams and flowcharts as visual storytelling
4. Monochromatic base with single accent color for maximum impact

**Color Philosophy:** Rich dark background (#09090B) with subtle warm undertone. Bright green accent (#22C55E) for sustainability and CTAs – the only saturated color. Zinc grays (#27272A, #3F3F46, #71717A) for layering. White (#FAFAFA) for text. The palette feels like a premium developer tool – serious, capable, modern.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-width sections with inner grid structure. Alternating dark/darker sections create depth. Services displayed in a precise 3-column grid with connecting lines. A "blueprint" aesthetic with faint grid lines visible in backgrounds.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Subtle dot-grid or line-grid patterns in section backgrounds
2. Glowing green accent borders and underlines on hover
3. Terminal/code-style text blocks for technical specifications

**Interaction Philosophy:** Precise, snappy transitions (200ms). Glow effects on hover. Border animations that trace card outlines. Keyboard-navigable with visible focus states.

**Animation:** Sharp fade-in (0.3s) with no transform – content appears crisply. Border-trace animations on cards. Typing effect for key headlines. Smooth number transitions.

**Typography System:** Display: "Geist" or "Satoshi" (700) – clean, technical, modern. Body: "Geist" (400) for consistency. Monospace: "Geist Mono" for data points and technical details.

## Entscheidung

**Gewählt: Idea 1 – "Atmospheric Altitude" (Aerospace Editorial Design)**

Begründung: Dieser Ansatz passt am besten zum B2B-Fokus in der General Aviation. Die editorial/magazine-artige Ästhetik vermittelt Professionalität und Vertrauen, während die Luftfahrt-inspirierten Elemente die Branchenzugehörigkeit unterstreichen. Die dunkle Farbpalette mit Teal-Akzenten hebt sich von typischen "grünen" Sustainability-Seiten ab und wirkt premium. Die asymmetrischen Layouts und cinematic Bildsprache eignen sich hervorragend für einen dynamischen Onepager mit B2B-Appeal.
